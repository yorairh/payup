# PayUp 0.5.0

Share a game live: friends follow the pot and the buy-ins from any phone, with nothing to install.

**New**
- **Share a live game.** On the table screen, tap the share icon to put the game online. A QR code and a link let friends follow the game from any phone or browser. Links look like https://payup.web.app/g/…
- **Your changes reach everyone live.** Buy-ins, cash-outs and the final result sync a moment after you make them. If you lose your connection, the top bar says "Offline" and the app keeps trying.
- **Watch a game.** Open a game link, or use "Watch a game" on the home screen and paste it. You see the pot, each player's buy-ins and cash-outs, live. Read-only.
- **Hand over hosting.** Pass the game to someone else, for example when the host has to leave: share the host link from "Hand over hosting". The new host can edit the game, and the old host switches to watching it live.
- **Links open in the app.** Tapping a game link on an Android phone with PayUp installed opens it in PayUp. Without the app it opens in the browser.
- **Finished games are kept.** When a game you watched ends, it is saved into your history and counts in your leaderboard.

**Changed**
- **Deleting a shared game removes its link.** When you delete a game you hosted from your history, the online copy goes too (this needs a connection). A game you only watched is deleted from your device only.
- **Starting a new game** also removes the online copy of the unfinished game you were hosting, when you are online.
- The app is a bit bigger to download (about 15 MB instead of 10) because of the online sharing.

**Good to know**
- Only games you tap Share on go online. Everything else stays on your device, as before.
- Anyone who has a game's link can watch it, and anyone who has its host link can take it over, so share the host link only with people you trust.
- A game stays online until you delete it from your history.
- Your history, leaderboard and friends stay on each device. Your saved games are untouched, and `PayUp-0.5.0.apk` installs over 0.4.0.
