# PayUp 1.0.0

Cash out in chips even when nobody knows what a chip is worth.

**New**
- **Chips without a set value.** When you count in chips on the cash-out screen, there is a new switch: "Chips have no set value". Turn it on, type how many chips each player finished with, and PayUp splits the pot by each player's share of all the chips. Everyone's win or loss comes out of the ratios, with nothing to agree on beforehand.
- **Live amounts.** The amounts appear as soon as every player has a chip count (0 is fine for a player who busted), and update as you type. "Pay up" waits until they are all in.
- **Rounding works the same.** The existing "round to whole shekels" switch applies here too, and the amounts always add up to the pot exactly.
- **In the results.** A player's details show their chips, the total, and their share of the pot, for example "50 of 200 chips = 25% of the pot".
- **Works everywhere.** The setting is saved with the game, kept in backups, and shared games show it to the people watching.

**Good to know**
- The existing mode, where you set the value of one chip, is unchanged.
- Your saved games are untouched, and `PayUp-1.0.0.apk` installs over 0.5.0.
